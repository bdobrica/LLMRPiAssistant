Setup and checks

Microphone checks stay on this device and are deleted after playback. Speak when the check starts; Stop cancels it. Unmute first to record. The test sound can play while muted.

Settings

Use this page only on your trusted home Wi-Fi. It has no sign-in or HTTPS. Changes take effect only when you press Save & apply.

Changing the city clears manual weather coordinates and uses the city's timezone. Existing reminders keep their scheduled instant.

Keys are never shown back. Saving a key does not verify its account access; try a voice question afterwards. Undo restores the previous keys and settings. Other reasoning providers need an integration before their keys can be used.

    If the clocks change at this time

      One-off reminders use the local chime and show their text here, even without Internet. Stop or the device button dismisses ringing timers and reminders. Microphone mute does not silence them.

      Timers

        Timers ring without Internet, including when the microphone is muted. Speaker volume still applies. Press the device button or Stop to dismiss a ringing timer. Alerts repeat for a limited time (two minutes by default); missed timers remain here.

        Hold the device button for 1.5 seconds to mute or unmute. Steady red means microphone capture is closed. A short press stops an answer and opens a correction window.

        These controls work without Internet on the same Wi-Fi. Voice requests need Internet. New conversation clears chat context; it does not delete logs, cancel apps or undo actions.
